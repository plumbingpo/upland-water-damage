Roof Leak Water Damage in Upland: What to Know

Where you see the stain and where the rain gets in are usually two different places. A loose shingle, a failed flashing joint or an aging seal around a skylight lets rain in, and it travels across the roof deck and framing until it falls into the insulation. By the time a brown ring shows on the ceiling, the attic above it has often been wet for a while. Locating all of the moisture and drying it is our job.

We are a water damage crew, not a roofing company. We work on the interior, which means removing wet insulation, drying drywall and framing, dealing with mold and rebuilding the ceiling. Should the leak still be active, our crew can cover that section with a tarp for now, or time our work with the roofing contractor you pick so nothing gets sealed over an open leak.

Do This First

Your First Steps

  1. Shut Off Wet CircuitsIf water is dripping through a light fixture, a ceiling fan or near an outlet, switch off that circuit at the breaker and leave the fixture alone.
  2. Contain the WaterSet buckets or pans under active drips and move furniture, rugs and electronics out of the way. Lay towels or plastic sheeting down to protect the floor.
  3. Drain a Bulging CeilingWhen water pools in a bubble overhead, pierce the low point with a screwdriver while standing clear, and let it run into a bucket.
  4. Stay Off the RoofClimbing on slick shingles or crawling around a dark attic is not worth the risk. From indoors, mark where the water enters, and let professionals handle everything else.
  5. Photograph the DamageBefore anything is moved or discarded, get video and photos of the attic, the ceiling and damaged items, and then phone us so drying can begin.
Will Insurance Pay?

What About Insurance?

Homeowners policies often cover interior water damage when a roof leak is sudden and accidental, such as wind tearing off shingles or a limb puncturing the roof. Insurers generally turn down claims tied to wear, age or lack of upkeep, and they may handle the roof repair on different terms than the inside damage. Photos and moisture readings from us go to the adjuster, though only your policy decides coverage, and your agent can explain it.

The Usual Culprits

How It Usually Starts

  • A valley liner that has worn through at the point where the most runoff passes.
  • Aging, cracked seals around a skylight frame that no longer keep water out.
  • A fallen limb or large hail that punctures or bruises the roof covering.
  • Fasteners working loose over time, so water follows the nail shank into the attic.
  • A vent pipe boot whose rubber has split from long sun exposure.
What Is at Risk

Where the Damage Spreads

Attic Insulation

For days after a leak, matted insulation sits wet on top of the ceiling and no longer insulates. In some cases fiberglass batts dry out and stay. Removal is the usual answer for wet cellulose.

Roof Framing

Repeated wetting makes decking, trusses and rafters turn dark, swell up and go soft. Once they rot, the frame supporting the roof is weaker.

Lights and Wiring

Recessed cans, ceiling fans and electrical boxes are low points where roof water collects and drips through. Moisture in a fixture can cause a short and later corrosion, which is why it needs inspection before use.

Inside the Walls

From the attic, a leak can drop into a stud bay and soak the wall insulation and drywall from behind. Bubbling paint or a swollen baseboard may be the only sign.

How We Fix It

What Happens After You Call

1

Trace the Leak Path

Moisture meters and a thermal camera let us follow the rain through the attic, the ceilings and the walls, so spots that look dry from underneath are not missed.

2

Cover and Protect

When rain can still get in, a temporary tarp from us or a call to your roofer comes first. Anything sitting beneath the damage, including the flooring, gets protected too.

3

Tear Out and Dry

Unsalvageable drywall and insulation come out, and dehumidifiers and air movers go into the attic space and each room under it, with readings taken until the framing has dried.

4

Sanitize and Repair

Where it is called for, we apply an antimicrobial to the bare framing. With the roof fixed, our team installs insulation and drywall, finishes the joints, and primes and paints so the repair blends in.
